Term schedule optional

The portal only publishes about a week of timetable, so it can't tell how many classes are left this term. Fill this in and the card switches from "you can skip 3 more right now" to "you can miss 6 of your 32 remaining classes" — and warns you if 75% stops being reachable.

Leave it off and everything still works; you just get the safe short-range number.

You almost certainly don't need this. A subject and its lab are paired automatically from their names — "ADA - B" with "ADA Lab 2 - B" — and that works for every subject on the portal today. Leave this empty.

Only if a subject shows up split in two, or two unrelated subjects get merged, add a row: the course name exactly as the portal shows it on the left, and the subject it should belong to on the right.
